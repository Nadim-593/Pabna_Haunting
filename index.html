The Whistling Ward

Block-C of the Pabna Mental Asylum was known locally as the "Hishor Ward" (Whistling Ward). Night workers reported a constant, inhuman whistling sound emanating from sealed rooms. Patients admitted to Block-C exhibited severe psychological deterioration within 72 hours — drawing the same symbol obsessively: a circle with an eye at center.

sOURCE: Pabna District Record, 1968

The Ichamati signal

Local fishermen on the Ichamati River report hearing radio broadcasts in unknown languages late at night — always between 02:00 and 03:17 local time. The broadcasts match no known frequency allocation. Direction-finding equipment consistently points toward the asylum ruins. Three HAM radio operators who attempted to record the signal went missing. Their equipment was found — operational — tuned to 7.743 MHz.

sOURCE: BTV Archive, 1989 | PPRC Field Report #023
